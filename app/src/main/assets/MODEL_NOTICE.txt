AgeMetr uses MiVOLO v2 (face-only input) for age and gender estimation.
The model runs on device with ONNX Runtime.

Model:   MiVOLO v2
Authors: Maksim Kuprashevich, Grigorii Alekseenko, Irina Tolstykh
Source:  https://huggingface.co/iitolstykh/mivolo_v2
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International
         (CC BY-NC 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
         Non-commercial use only.

Papers:
  M. Kuprashevich, I. Tolstykh. MiVOLO: Multi-input Transformer for Age and
  Gender Estimation. 2023. arXiv:2307.04616
  M. Kuprashevich, G. Alekseenko, I. Tolstykh. Beyond Specialization:
  Assessing the Capabilities of MLLMs in Age and Gender Estimation. 2024.
  arXiv:2403.02302

Changes made for AgeMetr (the file mivolo_face_int8.onnx is a modified
version of the original model):
  - converted to ONNX (opset 18), body input fixed to empty (face only);
  - quantized to int8 (dynamic quantization of MatMul weights).
Accuracy after conversion on 115 APPA-REAL photos: MAE 4.2 years,
gender 100 %.
